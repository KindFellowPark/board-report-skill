# Step 1: Story list and inputs, November 2026 report

What Jordan Lee (Executive Assistant, Office of Communications) gave Claude to start the
November 2026 President's Report to the Cedar Ridge College Board of Trustees. Everything
here is fictional.

## Month

November 2026. The report goes in the packet for the Board's November 19 meeting.

## Story list (newest and largest first)

1. **Allied Health Center groundbreaking** (cover story). October 22, east parking lot
   site. Dr. Helen Marsh, Board President Ruth Abernathy and Trustee Samuel Okonkwo spoke
   or attended. About 120 guests. Nursing student Marisol Ortega spoke. Two-story building,
   38,000 square feet, nursing, radiologic technology and medical assisting. Bond plus state
   capital outlay grant. Opens fall 2028. Nursing entering class grows from 40 to 64.
   Project cost: check with the finance office, I have seen two different figures.
2. **New dean.** Dr. Samira Qureshi starts November 3. Came from Linden Valley Community
   College, seven years running apprenticeship partnerships there. Dr. Marsh introduced her
   at the October 28 all-staff meeting. Title is in the HR announcement.
3. **Veterans Day recognition.** November 10, Veterans Resource Center. Trustee Teresa
   Valdivia (Navy veteran) gave challenge coins to the December graduates. A student
   veteran spoke about peer mentoring. Wall of service unveiled in the lounge. Enrollment
   figure for student veterans is in the center's fall report.
4. **Día de los Muertos ofrenda exhibit.** October 30 to November 2, Library Gallery. Fifth
   year. Eleven altars by Chicano studies, art and ceramics students. Ballet folklórico
   opened it. About 900 visitors, 14 elementary school classes toured. Trustee Carolyn
   Ishida attended the opening.
5. **Women's cross country conference title.** October 31 at Riverbend Park. First
   conference title since 2017. Kayla Nguyen won the individual race. Coach Darnell Price
   named Coach of the Year. Regionals November 15.
